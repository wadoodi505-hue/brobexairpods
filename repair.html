Knowledge

Common Questions

Do I need an appointment?

While walk-ins or physical drop-offs may be accommodated, starting with an online diagnostic request ensures priority attention.

Is the online estimate final?

No. All online estimates are purely conceptual and indicative. Exact pricing requires physical inspection of the hardware.

Do you use original parts?

We source premium replacement components prioritizing reliability, display accuracy, and battery chemistry. We do not fabricate unsupported OEM certifications.

Can my data be lost?

Data loss is rare for modular repairs (screens, batteries), but motherboard damage carries inherent risks. Always back up your device.

How should I handle water damage?

Turn the device off immediately. Do not charge it. Do not attempt to turn it on to "check if it works." Bring it in for a teardown.

How long can diagnosis take?

Standard visual assessments are fast, but intermittent logic board issues may require 2–5 business days for deep diagnosis.

What happens if it cannot be repaired?

If a device is deemed beyond economical repair or completely dead, we will outline your options, including potential data recovery efforts.
