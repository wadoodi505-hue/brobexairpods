Device Care

  • Backups: Always maintain cloud or local backups before any service.
  • Water Damage: Do not charge. Power off immediately and seek diagnosis.
  • Battery Health: Avoid extreme temperatures and deep discharging (0%).
  • Charging Issues: Often caused by lint buildup. Do not use metal tools to clean.
  • Cracked Screens: Apply clear tape over shattered glass to prevent injury before repair.
Knowledge

Common Questions

Do you use original parts?

We source premium replacement components. We do not make unsupported OEM certifications, but prioritize reliability, display accuracy, and battery chemistry.

Will I lose my data?

Data loss is rare for hardware repairs like screens or batteries, but software issues or motherboard damage carry risks. Back up your device prior to service.

How long does a repair take?

Standard repairs (screens, batteries) are often completed same-day. Diagnostics and complex logic board issues require 2-5 business days.
